## Ali Mahdi

PhD researcher in computational fluid mechanics and artificial intelligence at **CORIA**
(UMR 6614 CNRS), Université de Rouen Normandie.

[![ORCID](https://img.shields.io/badge/ORCID-0009--0005--0208--5479-A6CE39?logo=orcid&logoColor=white)](https://orcid.org/0009-0005-0208-5479)

I’m a PhD candidate at CORIA, Université de Rouen Normandie, working on wave–structure interactions using CFD and machine learning. My research combines OpenFOAM simulations with AI models to better understand and predict wave forces and overtopping at coastal structures.

My background is in mechanical engineering and fluid mechanics. Alongside my research, I teach and develop tools to make simulation and data analysis easier.

### Current work

**[COAST](https://github.com/alimahdiiii/COAST)** 
- Coastal Overtopping Analysis
and Simulation Toolkit. A Streamlit application that automates the whole OpenFOAM
pipeline for wave overtopping simulations, from parametric geometry and mesh
generation through to event-by-event post-processing.

**[A hybrid GAN-CNN surrogate framework for wave-structure interaction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oceaneng.2026.127494)**
- *Ocean Engineering*, 2026. A deep-learning surrogate that predicts free-surface
fields and hydrodynamic forces together rather than separately.

**[Efficient LES parametric studies via ANN-based multi-fidelity modeling and adaptive sampling](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10494-025-00669-0)**
- *Flow, Turbulence and Combustion*, 2025. Neural-network multi-fidelity models
that cut the cost of parametric LES campaigns.

**[OpenFOAM modelling of storm-induced wave run-up and swash hydrodynamics on a macrotidal beach](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7124090)**
- preprint, 2026. Run-up and swash hydrodynamics on a macrotidal beach under
storm conditions.
